The attribution maths above is deterministic, not AI — so we put Gemini to work on top of it. On the Money trail tab, Gemini reads the case and proposes hypotheses in plain language: a typology (fan-out, layering, round-trip, cash-out, mule reuse), a flow between two accounts, money still at this bank. Code checks every one against the same attribution bounds and graph — never the model.

Worked example — CASE-24051

Gemini: "At least ₹28,800 of the disputed money reached Sunil P." → Proven by the records — Rahul already held ₹1,200, so balance arithmetic forces it.

Investigator asks the ledger: "Did at least ₹25,000 reach Sunil?" → Gemini turns the question into a check; code answers from the records, in one sentence it wrote itself.

This is the direct answer to "the innovation isn't AI": the hardest, most original part of the product (the amount ranges) is now also where Gemini does its most tightly-verified work. Nothing it proposes reaches the investigator unchecked, and it never does the arithmetic.
