The customer raises it. The bank receives a ready case.

Customers sign in to their own portal and raise a dispute by chat or form. They choose from their own payments and fixed answers, so every dispute maps cleanly to the right customer, account, payment and category.

  • Lands in the bank’s queue as a New case with SLA clocks and priority
  • A free-text "tell us in your own words" entry: Gemini suggests the answers, the customer confirms each one
  • OTPs, PINs and card numbers typed by the customer are masked
  • Plain-language progress, deadlines and file uploads for the customer
  • Customers never see risk signals, other accounts or AI reports
  • Ask Demo Bank: a Gemini FAQ/safety assistant, scoped to banking and disputes, citing help articles
Customer interaction improvement is a named focus of the BFSI track. The intake is deliberately guided rather than free chat, so nothing the customer says can be misfiled.
