Hallucinations are caught, flagged and measured

  • Every cited record ID must exist in the snapshot
  • Every ₹ amount and time must match a cited record
  • No “proves fraud”, “customer is liable” or “reject the claim”
  • Flow of the disputed funds is never marked verified
  • Gemini judge checks each claim against its records

Every report records its hallucination rate, confidence, cost, tokens, snapshot hash and trace ID.

Flagged claims are kept and shown in red, never silently dropped, so the investigator sees exactly what the model got wrong. Click any citation chip to open the record it points to.
