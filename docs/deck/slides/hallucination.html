Hallucinations are caught, flagged and measured

• Every cited record ID must exist in the snapshot

• Every ₹ amount and time must match a cited record

• No “proves fraud”, “customer is liable” or “reject the claim”

• A Gemini judge fact-checks each claim against its records

Every report records its hallucination rate, confidence, cost, tokens, snapshot hash and trace ID. The fact-check can be re-run on its own.

Flagged claims are kept and shown, never silently dropped. The investigator reviews each section and can accept or reject every sentence before recording a decision.
