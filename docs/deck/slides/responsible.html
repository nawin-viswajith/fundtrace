No black-box trust score

Six dimensions stay separate: risk signals, completeness, consistency, traceability, policy eligibility and AI confidence.

  • AI down, timed out or over budget: “Not analysed”, never a guess
  • The AI suggests; only the investigator decides
  • Every action is in a hash-chained audit trail
  • Synthetic data only, labelled on every screen
Incomplete evidence is neither fraudulent nor legitimate — it is incomplete. Keeping the dimensions apart stops one weak signal from deciding a customer’s case.
