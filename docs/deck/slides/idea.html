FundTrace gives the investigator three things

Follow the money

The observable downstream trail, rebuilt from ledger records in time order, with how much of each hop could be the disputed funds.

Show the proof

Every fraud signal lists the facts and record IDs behind it. Every report sentence cites a record you can open.

Mark the guesses

Confirmed, derived, inferred, unknown and contradictory are labelled everywhere. Inferred is never shown as fact.

This is the whole product in one slide. Everything that follows is one of these three ideas made concrete.
