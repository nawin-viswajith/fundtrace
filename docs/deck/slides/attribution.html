“The amounts add up” is not evidence

Rahul’s account already held ₹1,200 when the disputed ₹50,000 arrived. When ₹30,000 left for Sunil, how much of it was the disputed money?

FundTrace answers with balance arithmetic under four standard tracing methods. A positive lower bound is derived: the ledger forces it. An upper bound alone is only inferred.

Money is conserved under every method, and our tests check it. Attribution never shows who controlled the funds or why.

This is the innovation judges should remember. Most tools stop at “these amounts match”. We compute what the ledger actually forces, show the range, and label it honestly.
