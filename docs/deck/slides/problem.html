A disputed payment is a race against the clock

01 · Speed

Money moves in minutes

Real-time payments are split and forwarded soon after they land. In our headline scenario, ₹50,000 reaches three more accounts within an hour.

02 · Tracing

Trails are pieced together by hand

Investigators read statements account by account. Amounts that “add up” are easily mistaken for proof of where the money went.

03 · Liability

Blind automation is a risk

US regulators fined Bank of America $225 million in 2022, partly for relying on an automated fraud filter instead of investigating claims.

Source for 03: US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and OCC enforcement actions, July 2022. Scenario figures are synthetic.

Three pressures at once: speed, manual tracing, and the liability of letting automation decide. The answer is not “more automation” — it is AI that investigates and explains, with a human deciding.
